# hundred-doors: models brief for the Model Builder

Story: "100 DOORS. ONE WISH. I'M ON DOOR 50." Jake approved the script and the voice (Wed 11-25); `script.txt` and the final voice are in this folder. **Start after the Sphinx pack is pushed.** Which video builder films it gets decided later, so make the packs ready to drop into any of our Blender scenes.

**Style:** match the locked Reflection cast (rounded heads, bright and clean, Moth Wings / Stag quality). Read the Studio Bible and `LUNCHBOX-CAST-GUIDE`. Push one still per model to `proofs/hundred-doors/` and KEEP BUILDING (no approval step; Claude reviews them). Deliver each finished pack the same way as the Egg Army packs. Delete temp renders after you push (the PC disk filled up on 11-25).

## New models (3)
1. **The Key Thief** (the main villain of this part). A skinny grown man in a long black hooded cloak covered in hundreds of tiny jingling keys (gold, silver, bronze). He holds one big golden key that glows. Sly grin, sharp eyebrows. Rig for: laughing, turning the key in the air like unlocking a door, stepping through a door, screaming and dropping to his knees.
   - The **golden key** must be its own object that can **snap in two**.
   - The **cloak keys** must be separate pieces (or one instanced group) so they can **crumble to dust** all at once.
2. **The giant armor** (door 7). An empty suit of medieval plate armor the size of a barn, dented and dusty, with warm light glowing through the helmet slit and the gaps in the joints. Nobody inside. Rig for a heavy chasing run (big stomping steps).
3. **The crown in the dark** (the last 7 seconds). A golden crown that glows, floating in front of a very tall shadow figure. No face is ever shown, only the crown and a hint of shoulders. It needs to read in near-black lighting.

## Effects (use our existing cast)
- **Turning to stone:** a gray stone material with cracks that creeps up the hero kid's legs from the feet, then reverses back to normal. Make it a reusable material/shader driver.
- **The statue garden:** 6 to 10 of our usual cast posed mid-action and turned to gray stone, with **surprised faces** (open mouths, wide eyes). Add moss on a few so it looks like years.

## Sets and props
- **The castle on the hill:** a wide hero shot, plus its big wooden gate, which slams shut.
- **Numbered doors:** big stone doors with carved numbers (1, 7, 30, 41, 49, 50, 60, 80, 100).
- **Door 1:** floating stepping stones over a bottomless pit. The stones sink when stood on.
- **Door 7:** a tall stone maze, big enough for the giant armor.
- **Door 50:** a torch-lit stone hall with stairs going up and a window looking down at the hero's farm (candle in a window, sister on the fence).
- **Floor trapdoor:** opens under the hero.
- **A door in thin air:** a door frame with golden light that the thief opens and steps out of.
- **Props:** Dad's old wooden sword, half a loaf of bread, a bed for Dad.
