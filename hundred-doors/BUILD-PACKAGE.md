## Jake's quality notes from 11-25 (all apply here)
- **Steady camera.** Lock the camera in each shot: no drifts, orbits or whips. The energy comes from the cuts: a new shot about every 1 to 1.5 s, each showing exactly what the voice says.
- **Realistic movement.** No constant-speed moves. Every action gets anticipation, ease-in and ease-out, overshoot and settle, and holds. Different speeds: the kid is quick and light, the armor is slow and crushing, and the thief is slippery and fast.
- **Cinematic angles.** Wide establishing shots, low angles on the armor and the castle, close-ups on faces, depth of field.
- **Face variety.** Every person has a clearly different face and hair. The kid's face changes with each beat: determined, panicked, cocky, terrified, triumphant.
- Bright daylight outside. Torch-warm inside the castle, never murky; only the final 20 s goes dark.
