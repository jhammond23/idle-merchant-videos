In this town, every kid touches the Power Stone at ten, and it shows your power level.
I'm the kid who sweeps the throne room. This year, I broke the stone.
Most kids get a ten. The King is ten thousand. Nobody has ever beaten him.
The Prince went first. Five thousand. The whole court cheered.
Then he pointed his crown at me. "Let the sweeper try. Maybe it'll say one."
The whole room laughed. Even the King.
I touched the stone.
It said zero.
The Prince laughed so hard he fell down the throne steps.
Then the zero turned into a one. Ten. A hundred.
A thousand. Five thousand. Past the Prince.
Ten thousand. Past the King.
The numbers blurred into light. The stone cracked.
Then it showed one shape. A sideways eight.
And the Power Stone exploded. Every piece floated up, and turned to face me.
The whole court gasped. Except the King.
"It only says zero when the number is too big to count," he said. "The last time was the first king of this town."
He'd known since I was born. That's why he made me the sweeper. So I'd never touch that stone.
"Guards! Take him!"
A hundred guards drew their swords.
I lifted one hand, like I was swatting a fly.
Every sword in the room bent in half. One guard's armor fell right off. He was wearing pajamas underneath.
The Prince was the last one holding a sword. I looked at it. It bent too.
The King spent ten years keeping me away from that stone.
It took me one second to break it.
What's the first thing you'd do with infinite power?
