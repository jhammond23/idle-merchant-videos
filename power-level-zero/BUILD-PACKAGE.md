## The idea
The story follows "The Black Knight Knelt to Me" beat for beat: a ceremony, the lowest kid in the room, the Prince's insult, a slow reveal, the King's twist, a hundred guards, and a punch line. The hook is a **giant glowing number counting up on screen**, the way Black Knight's 40-step countdown worked.

## Cast (locked Reflection cast only, rounded heads)
- **The sweeper (lead), about 10:** the burnt-orange hero kid in a plain apron, with a broom. Calm, almost bored, never scared.
- **The Prince:** smug, gold crown, fancy clothes.
- **The King:** older, bearded, heavy crown, on the throne.
- **The court:** nobles and ten-year-old kids in a line for Testing Day.
- **A hundred royal guards** in polished armour. One of them must be ready to lose his armour and stand there in striped pajamas.

## Set and props
- A grand throne room: gold, red banners, torches and tall windows, deep colour (house look, saturation about 0.55).
- **The Power Stone:** a waist-high slab of dark crystal on a pedestal in the middle of the hall. A glowing number floats above it when touched.
- **The counter:** a huge glowing number floating over the stone, in the same golden font every time. It must be readable at phone size.

## Frame 0 (cover)
The sweeper's hand on the Power Stone. The stone is cracking open, white light blasting out, and a giant glowing **∞** floats above it. The whole court is frozen behind him: the Prince mid-laugh, the King leaning forward. Title: **MY POWER LEVEL SAID ZERO.** Handle top left; Flashloop card top right (sponsor edition only).

## Camera
About 1 s shots in the Moth Wings style: eye-level medium shots and big faces, low angles on the King, wide shots only when a new place appears. The camera barely moves inside a shot. No whips or shake; Claude adds the shake-in and the punches in the finishing pass.

## Sound
No music bed. Effects only: crowd cheers and laughter, the rising hum of the counter (pitch climbing with the number), the crack, the explosion, glass shards tinkling, swords drawn, the bent-sword "boing", armour clattering. Silence on "It said zero."
