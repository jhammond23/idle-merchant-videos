# Character style change (Jake, Tue 11-24, 7:52 AM)

Jake watched Slime World and rejected the look of the people. His words:

> "It was done in the wrong animation style. It was done in like the Coraline style animation. I kind of want to ask if we could go back to the same animation style as we used in the reflection [knelt] to me rather than the animation style that we've been doing, but I want to keep the same quality. I just want the people themselves to be in that animation style."

## What this means

See `character-styles-A-B-C.jpg` in this folder.

- **A is the style Jake wants: "My Reflection Knelt to Me."** Reference film on main: `next-stories-1114/my-reflection-knelt-to-me-part1.mp4`. Classic Roblox block people: a plain block head, block torso, arms and legs with flat sides, a simple classic Roblox face, simple hair, plain flat-colour clothes.
- **C (Slime World) is rejected:** round puppet heads, big glossy eyes, blush, sculpted hair clumps, soft bevelled bodies. Do not use these people again.
- **B (Zombies, Black Knight, Phoenix: block bodies with the anime faces and sculpted hair) is what we have been doing.** Jake is being asked whether finished B films are fine. Until he answers, do not start anything new in B.

## The rule from now on

1. **People are style A in every new build and every rebuild that has not started rendering.**
2. **Everything else stays at today's quality:** the deep colour and strong light of the house look, the sets, the creatures, the effects, the camera rules, the sound, the captions, the sponsor ending. Only the people change.
3. Creatures are not people. Tiny the slime, the dragon, the wolf, the Phoenix and the stags keep their current look.
4. The lead still needs to stand out: one bright outfit colour nobody else wears, light on the face, and acting through pose, head tilt and timing.
5. Before a full render in style A, push one still of the main cast to `proofs/<story>/` so Claude can show Jake.
