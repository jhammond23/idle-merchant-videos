In this town, every kid trades their arms once at the Gate of Oaths, forever.
The gate keeps your old arms in its stone and burns a number on your wrists: how rare your new ones are.
Half the kids get stone arms. One in two.
I traded mine for lightning. One in ten million.
But the Warlord who rules our town has a law: the rarest arms in town wear the iron crown.
His iron arms were one in a million. So my lightning would take his crown.
He couldn't steal my arms. The gate's chains grab anyone who tries and turn their arms to stone.
And if anyone tries, they say the gate itself wakes up.
So he chained my little sister to his war beast, a horned giant that kneels only to the rarest arms in sight.
That morning, he rode to my door. "Leave by midnight, or she gets dragged away."
"Your beast kneels to me," I said, and my sparks shoved him back a step.
At midnight, I stood at the Gate of Oaths, and the whole town came with torches.
The Warlord rode up with his beast, my sister chained behind it.
"Then it drags you both," he said, and let it charge.
So I raised my arms, and lightning split the ground in front of it.
The beast skidded. Then it knelt to me, and the whole town went quiet.
Its chains went slack, and my sister ran free.
The Warlord jumped down. "Those arms are mine!"
He grabbed my wrists to tear the lightning off.
So the gate's chains snapped around his wrists, and his iron arms turned to stone.
The whole town laughed. Stone arms. One in two.
"Kneel to me!" he roared at his beast.
But it stayed kneeling to me. Mine were still the rarest arms in sight.
So by his own law, the town pulled off his iron crown and laid it at my feet.
Then the Gate of Oaths shook, because someone had tried to break its oath.
It stood up on legs and arms made of every arm it ever kept.
It towered over the castle.
The war beast turned and bowed to it.
And a number began to burn over its head.
What's the number over the gate? Guess.
