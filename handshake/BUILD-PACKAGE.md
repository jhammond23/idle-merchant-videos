## Cast (locked Reflection cast, rounded heads)
- **The farm kid (lead), about 10 to 12:** the burnt-orange hero kid in farm clothes. Confident, cheeky grin.
- **The blacksmith, the archer, the knight:** three quick skill-givers.
- **Great fighters:** a line of big knights and fighters, laughing at the kid as they shake his hand.
- **The king:** nailing the poster to the gate.
- **The Iron Champion:** a huge knight in dark iron full plate (no face shown, a visor with a glowing slit), a giant sword, "1,000 duels" scratched into his shield. Built from the locked-cast body under armour; no new face needed.
- **The arena crowd.**

## The visual motif (it carries the twist)
Every handshake: **golden light runs from the other person's hand up the kid's arm**, with a small icon of the skill (hammer, arrow, sword) popping over his head. At the twist, show the truth: the light was flowing OUT of them. **Their hands go grey and start shaking.** Show it on the champion, then on every knight who laughed.

## Frame 0 (cover)
The kid shaking the Iron Champion's huge gauntlet in the arena, golden light pouring up the kid's arm, the champion's sword already on the ground and his hand trembling. Title: **I COPY ANY SKILL WITH ONE HANDSHAKE.** Handle top left.
