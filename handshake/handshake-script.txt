In this town, one kid could copy any skill with a single handshake. Me.
Shake a blacksmith's hand, and I could swing a hammer all day.
An archer's, and I never missed.
A knight's, and I fought like ten men.
At first, I thought it was the best power in the kingdom.
So I shook hands with every great fighter who rode through town.
Most of them laughed at the farm kid with the big ideas.
By twelve, I was better than men who'd trained their whole lives.
People started calling me the Little Champion.
Then the king nailed a poster to the town gate.
Beat his Iron Champion, undefeated in a thousand duels, and win a chest of gold.
I signed up that second.
In the arena, the Iron Champion was already waiting.
I shook his gauntlet, and just like always, I knew every move he had.
But before the bell even rang, he dropped his sword. "I yield."
The whole arena went silent. I didn't understand.
Then I saw his hand. It was shaking so hard he couldn't pick his sword back up.
That's when I understood.
I wasn't copying skills. I was taking them.
And every knight who ever laughed at me had already shaken my hand.
