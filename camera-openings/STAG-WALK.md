## What "walking" means here

- **Every shot where a stag's body moves across the ground gets a real walk.** No shot may show the body travelling while the hooves stay planted, or hooves sliding along the ground.
- **A four-beat walk**, like a real deer or horse: left hind, left front, right hind, right front. Each leg lifts, swings forward in an arc, and plants.
- **Planted hooves do not move at all** until they lift again. Match the stride length to how fast the body travels, so the feet never skate.
- **Heavy and slow, because they are huge:** the black stag takes about one full stride cycle every 1.6 to 2 seconds; the Mountain is slower, about every 2.5 to 3 seconds.
- **Weight:** the body rises and dips slightly with each step, the head bobs with the stride, the crowns on the antlers sway, and each plant kicks up a small puff of dust or splash.
- **The voice track stays identical.** You may move the existing hoof-thud effects so they land on the plants.
