# Creature build sheet: THE CROWN STAG (called "the Kingtaker" in the story)

## What it is
A giant black war stag. Every time it beats another town's war beast, it takes that town's crown and wears it on its antlers. It carries ninety-nine. It is the enemy's unbeatable beast, and in the story it kneels to a small kid. So it must look terrifying from far away and noble up close. Not evil.

## The one-second read (most important)
On a phone, with no sound, a stranger should see: a huge black stag, antlers like a bare winter tree, covered in glittering gold crowns. That silhouette is the whole point. Protect it in every shot.

## Size
- The shoulder is as high as a church roof. The antlers add half as much again.
- The kid stands about as tall as its hoof.

## Body
- Heavy and thick like a giant elk, not a slim deer. Deep chest, thick neck, a shaggy mane down the throat.
- Coat: matte charcoal black with a few old pale scars.
- Dark iron armour on the chest and forehead, with rivets. Worn and dented.
- A few torn red war banners tied around the neck and one foreleg.
- Breath steams in cold air.

## Face
- A real fur face, readable on a phone. No skull, no mask, no horror.
- Eyes glow pale gold. Calm and steady, like it is judging you.
- It needs three looks: a hard war stare, soft (when it kneels), and a protective glare.

## Antlers and crowns
- Antlers: very wide and tall, branching like a dead oak. Bone-white at the base, darkening to black at the tips.
- 99 gold crowns hang on the branches. Mixed shapes and sizes, some dented, a few with red or blue jewels. Make them bigger than real crowns so they read as crowns on a phone, not as dots.
- The crowns swing and catch the light when it moves.
- One small crown sits loose on a low branch near the brow. It must be a separate piece that can slide down, drop from branch to branch and land on the kid's head (too big for him: it sits crooked over one eyebrow).
- All 99 crowns must be able to slide down the antlers and pile up around the kid in one shot.

## Moves needed
1. A slow heavy walk, crowns swaying.
2. A charge, then a dead stop a step from the kid, hooves ploughing the ground.
3. The kneel: front legs fold, antlers lower all the way to the ground.
4. Neck laid flat on the ground like a ramp so the kid can run up it.
5. Standing tall and walking with the kid riding between the antlers.
6. Protective stance: standing over the kid with a hoof on each side of him, head low, antlers forward.
7. Head turned away (he snubs the enemy king).

## Sound
- The crowns chime softly, like metal wind chimes, when it walks.
- Deep hoof thuds. One long low breath before it kneels.

## Colour and light
- Black, bone-white and gold, with small red accents.
- It must work in bright daylight (a golden morning, red banners on the field). Add a gold rim light so the black body never turns into a blob.

## Original design only
Do not base it on any existing game, film or show creature.

## The second creature: the Mountain (needed for the last ten seconds of Part 1 and all of Part 2)
The enemy king's first war beast: the same stag shape at the size of a mountain.
- Mossy stone-grey coat, with earth, rocks and small living trees still on its back and shoulders from lying asleep for years.
- Antlers like a dead grey forest, hung with thousands of crowns (nine thousand in the story: instanced gold points are fine at this size, with real crowns only where the camera gets close).
- Eyes: two huge slow gold lamps.
- Lying down, its outline must pass for a long forested hill behind the castle.
- Moves: stand up out of the hillside (earth and trees sliding off); raise one front hoof and bring it down to a dead stop just above the kid; kneel with its chin on the ground; lay its neck across the valley like a road; a slow walk seen from far away.
- Sound: from far underground, like a ship's horn mixed with a landslide.
