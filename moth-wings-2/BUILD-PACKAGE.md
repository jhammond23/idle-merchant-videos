## Style (same as Part 1)
- A new shot every 3 to 4 words: **about 70 to 80 shots**, average 0.9 to 1.1 s, nothing over 1.8 s.
- Literal pictures; mostly eye-level medium shots and close-ups with **big faces**; low angles on the powerful thing.
- Still camera inside shots. **No shake, zooms or whips** (Claude adds the shake-in, whoosh and impact effects).
- **Bright and saturated, average brightness 135 or more.** The Great Fog is **glowing white-gold daylight fog**, soft and luminous, never grey or murky. The kids are silhouettes in it until the eye-spot light finds them.
- Captions as Part 1: small white, lower middle, one red word.
- No music bed: wing flaps, wind, the fog's hush, a cow moo, the duke's echoing shout, crowd laughter, a warm shimmer when the eye spots light, thousands of fluttering wings at the end.

## Cast
- **Me:** the Part 1 hero (locked-cast `kid`, burnt-orange shirt) with the moth wings: small and folded when grounded; open, they are big and soft grey with **two eye spots that glow warm gold like lanterns** and light the fog around him.
- **The duke:** a grown man, locked-cast body, rich purple and gold robes, a big curled moustache, **the biggest griffin-gold wings in the kingdom**, smug face. Clearly the duke's son's father (same colours). Comic, never scary.
- **The first-year:** the same small girl from Part 1 (scale 0.6, `hero_pixie` hair, tiny sparrow wings). Scared face, then a small amazed smile.
- **Crowd kids:** griffin, eagle and sparrow kids from Part 1. **One sparrow kid on a cow.**
- **The jar:** a big glass jar with a cork lid **punched with little holes**. It appears twice: the duke hands it to me; I hand it back.

## Opening (frame 0, like Part 1's fly-past; Jake wants the same attention grab)
Shot 1 (0 to 0.89 s, "In this town,"): **I dive out of the bright white fog straight at the camera**, wings fully open, both eye spots blazing gold, and **shoot right past the lens in the first 0.3 s** (wings sweeping across the whole frame, motion blur, sparks of gold light), then the fog swirls closed behind me. It must be the most striking image of the film. Then line 1 continues: kids with wings in the square; yesterday's eclipse (my huge open wings blocking the sun over the cliff, the crowd in shadow, as in Part 1's last shot).
