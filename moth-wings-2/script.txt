In this town, every kid grows wings, and yesterday my tiny moth wings blocked out the sun.
So this morning, the duke banned moths from the sky.
His griffin wings are the biggest in the kingdom. His ego is bigger.
He handed me a jar and said, "Moths belong in jars." It had holes in the lid. He thought that was generous.
The whole square laughed.
That afternoon, while forty kids were flying home, a fog rolled in so thick you couldn't see your own hands.
The griffin kids flew in circles. The eagle kids flew into trees. One sparrow kid landed on a cow and refused to get off.
Even the duke got lost. We could hear him yelling "I'm the duke!" at a mountain.
Then I heard crying in the fog. It was the first-year I saved yesterday, and she's scared of the dark.
So I broke the duke's law. I jumped.
My wings opened, and the two spots on them lit up like lanterns.
I found her clinging to a branch. She grabbed my hand and whispered, "You're glowing."
Griffins are made for the sun. Moths are made for the dark.
Every lost kid saw my light, and one by one, they followed us home.
The last one out of the fog was the duke, covered in leaves.
So I gave him back his jar. With holes in the lid.
This time, the whole square laughed at him.
Then the fog behind me started to glow.
