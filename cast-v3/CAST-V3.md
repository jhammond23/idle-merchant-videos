# Cast v3, phases 1+2: the new cast on one body kit (Claude, Thu 11-27)

Built for "In this town" from the research report *Kids story short character designs*: phase 1 = rows 1-8 of its build table, phase 2 = rows 9-20 (all built, including row 11, the Boss Giant rig, see the end of this doc). Everything is free: procedural geometry and shaders in Blender (no paid generation), faces drawn with our own `faces.py` tooling or reused from the channel's own packs.

- **`cast-v3.blend`** (Blender 5.3, compressed, textures packed): one collection per character, `Cast v3 | <Name>`. **File > Append > Collection.** The characters stand side by side in the file; after appending, set the rig's Location X back to 0 (or wherever you want them).
- **`glb/<key>.glb`**: backups for Jake's Blender 5.1 (5.3 files may not open in 5.1). Mesh, armature, bone-parented parts, plain colours, the default face baked to a texture. Every expression PNG is in `glb/faces/<set>/`, every pose (quaternions) in `glb/poses/<key>.json` and in the glb's extras.
- **`lineup.png`** (two rows: phase 1 and phase 2, each with Cast v2's Hero kid and Little sister for scale), **`silhouette.png`** (outline test, both rows at one scale, incl. a 128 px thumbnail test), **`sheets/<key>.png`** (turnaround, poses, expressions, specs per character).
- Phase 2 adds `colour_check.py` (the CIELAB check, output `scratch/colour_check.txt`), `p2run.py` (the memory-safe job runner used for every Blender job) and `sheets/boss_giant_scale.png`. To rebuild one character into the library: `BASE=cast-v3.blend ONLY=<key> SAVE=cast-v3.blend python3 -I cast_v3.py` (keys: herald, horde, horde_slate/_ash/_moss/_frost, old_legend, golden_sister, roll_merchant, master_smith, stone_keeper, rival_heir, turned_friend, mirror_twin, jester, boss_giant).
- Source: `cast_v3.py` (characters, data records) on `v3kit.py` (the kit), faces from `faces_v3.py`, renders from `render_v3.py`, sheets from `compose_sheets.py`, backups from `export_glb.py`, helpers in `cast_v3_tools.py` (also inside the .blend as a text block).

Same units as Cast v2: 1 m = 3.3 BU, Hero kid = 4.16 BU tall with a 0.96 BU head. **HH** below is the report's hero-head unit, which assumed the hero is 3.25 heads tall, so 1 HH = 4.16 / 3.25 = 1.28 BU. The real Cast v2 hero is 4.33 of his own heads, so the kit keeps the report's *height ratios to the hero* and its head multipliers (every person's head is 0.82-1.0x the hero's), not the report's head-count percentages.

Heads: the Reflection cylinder (most), the classic Roblox head (Squire, Turned Friend, yellow crowd) and, new in phase 2, a bevelled **block** head (`make_head(..., kind='block')`, the Horde).

**Hand props** (phase 2): modelled where they belong in the default pose, moved into the hand bone's rest space, bone-parented, so they follow every pose. In poses the prop wasn't designed for they can look odd (the Herald's trumpet in `fists_up`, the Golden Sister's trophy in `arms_crossed`); the sheets' pose rows double as the clipping test.

**Parts** (Roblox R15 names, rigid, each bone-parented to its bone): `Head_Geo, UpperTorso_Geo, LowerTorso_Geo, Left/Right UpperArm_Geo, LowerArm_Geo, Hand_Geo, UpperLeg_Geo, LowerLeg_Geo, Foot_Geo` (+ `Neck_Geo` on long necks). Clothing is body texture plus thin shells (tunic skirts, breastplates, vambraces), capes are lofted sheets with Solidify, hair is rounded blobs (library hair or our own spikes).

**Bones** (16, R15 names; character faces -Y, *Left = the character's own left = +X*): `Root > LowerTorso > UpperTorso > Head`, `UpperTorso > Left/RightUpperArm > LowerArm > Hand`, `LowerTorso > Left/RightUpperLeg > LowerLeg > Foot`. Ember: `Root, LowerTorso, UpperTorso, Head, Left/RightUpperArm > Hand, Left/RightUpperLeg > Foot, Tail > TailTip`.
**Sockets** (empties, Roblox `_Att` names): `Hat_Att, FaceCenter_Att, BodyFront_Att, BodyBack_Att, Waist_Att, LeftGrip_Att, RightGrip_Att, LeftShoulder_Att, RightShoulder_Att`. Parent a prop to the socket (Child Of or plain parenting) and it follows the pose.

**Textured clothing**: shared node groups `CV3 Cloth` (over-under weave bump, mottling, darker hem band with a stitched line, optional stitched front seam), `CV3 Leather` (scuffs, creases, varying sheen), `CV3 Metal` (streak scratches, roughness breakup). Hem and seam positions are object properties (`hem_z`, `hem_h`, `seam_x`), so one material serves every garment. Change a colour on the group node of a `CV3 ...` material.

## Poses
Every rig has `idle, point, laugh, shocked, fists_up, arms_crossed` (no kneel, on purpose: viewers called out the kneel), plus `whisper / gasp / cheer / drop` on the crowd and `roar` on Ember. Phase 2 extras (the first one is the default): Herald `trumpet, proclaim`; Horde `roar, hunch`; Old Legend `hunched, stand_tall, raise_sword`; Golden Sister `trophy, trophy_up`; Roll Merchant `welcome, offer`; Master Smith `shoulder, strike`; Stone Keeper `present, raise_stone`; Rival Heir `ready, en_garde`; Mirror Twin `mirror_cross`; Jester `puppet, bow`; Boss Giant `stomp, reach, roar`. The Mirror Twin and the Turned Friend open in `idle` (the Turned Friend's idle is his colder stance). Each character opens in its own default (First Rank chin-up hand-on-hip, Rigger hunched showing the rune, Squire nervous hands, crowd in their fixed reaction).
- **Pose Library**: every pose is also an Action asset `<Name> | pose <pose>` (catalog "Cast v3"). Add `cast-v3.blend` as an asset library to drag poses from the asset shelf.
- **Script** (works on appended characters, the poses travel inside the rig's `poses` property): run the `cast_v3_tools.py` text block, then `set_pose(bpy.data.objects['First Rank | rig'], 'point', frame=12)`. If a rig has `pose_props` (the Old Legend), `set_pose` also sets them: `raise_sword` puts `sword` = 1, every other pose puts it back to 0.
- By hand: select the rig, Pose Mode, rotate bones as usual (quaternions).

## Expressions, talking, blinks
Select the **rig** and use Object Properties > Custom Properties (drivers pass them to the head):
- `face` = expression index (the names are in the rig's `face_names` property and in the table above). Key it with **Constant** interpolation.
- `mouth` = 0 expression's own mouth, 1 closed, 2 half, 3 open (First Rank, Rigger, Squire, True-Form). `blink` = 1 closes the eyes. `glow` = visor / eye glow strength (Oathbound and Jailer default 1; True-Form default 0, set 1 for the glowing-eye power moment).
- Helpers in `cast_v3_tools.py`: `set_face(rig, 'shocked', frame)`, `talk(rig, 'open', frame)`, `blink(rig, frame)`, `lipsync(rig, amplitudes, start)` (per-frame loudness from the ElevenLabs audio -> closed / half / open, each state held 2+ frames).
- Ember: `reskin` 0/1/2 recolours body, belly, horns and the tail flame together.
- Phase 2 rig properties (Object Properties > Custom Properties on the rig, key them like `face`): Old Legend `sword` (0 planted beside him, 1 in his right hand); Roll Merchant `rarity` (chest glow 0 common grey, 1 uncommon green, 2 rare blue, 3 epic violet = default, 4 legendary gold); Stone Keeper `class` (crystal 0 unread white-blue, 1 legendary gold, 2 warrior red, 3 ranger green, 4 mage violet, 5 weakest dim grey); Jester `puppet` (0 closed, 1 open: key it to make the puppet talk); Turned Friend `glow` (1 magenta eye on, 0 = turned back); Boss Giant `skin` 0-2, `weak` 0..1, `horns` / `crown` / `chains` 0/1. Glowing eye overlays now close with `blink` (kit fix in phase 2; the phase-1 True-Form keeps its old shader until it is rebuilt).
- `mouth` talk sets: every phase-2 person has closed / half / open (the storybook ones were drawn in the pack's style, new). The Horde has none.

## Rules (same as Cast v2)
- Mix them: no two people in a shot share face style, skin and body. The Crowd Six are for every "everyone laughed / gasped / cheered" beat; duplicate them as collection instances for bigger crowds.
- The Oathbound never removes the helmet; the Jailer never shows a face. The True-Form Lead is the Hero kid, never a new person.
- Never an all-white robe with an upright pointed hood: it reads as a hate-group costume. The Stone Keeper's hood and mantle are indigo with the point curled back; keep it that way in any reskin.
- The Golden Sister is never cruel; the Rival Heir is never romance; the Turned Friend is a rescue arc, not a horror figure.
- Only Claude creates new characters (Jake's rule).

## Row 11, the Boss Giant rig
**Built.** Collection `Cast v3 | The Boss Giant`, sheet `sheets/boss_giant.png` (with a VARIANTS row), scale check `sheets/boss_giant_scale.png`, backup `glb/boss_giant.glb`.
- One giant body for every huge threat: 16.64 BU = 4.0x the Hero kid (13 HH), small block head (14 % of its height), plated shoulders about 3 of its head widths, pillar legs, open hands big enough to stand on. Overflows the top of a 9:16 frame whenever the Hero kid is full-height in the same shot.
- Rig switches: `skin` 0 iron and rust (default), 1 storm grey, 2 blue fire (colours and seam glow change together); `weak` 0..1 = the glowing weak point in its chest (0 = broken / defeated); `horns`, `crown`, `chains` 0/1 (chains on by default) so each story's giant looks new.
- Face: no face, glowing eyes + carved brows: 0 glare, 1 roar, 2 hurt. Poses: shared 6 + `stomp` (default), `reach` (palm out), `roar`.
- Rigid kit limits show most on the giant: the chains follow the forearms rigidly (they swing up with raised arms), and there's no IK for planting its feet.
