## Ground rules

**Ground rules (Studio Bible, 11-25 evening):** lock the camera inside each shot and cut about every 1-1.5 s, with slow moves only on lines 15 and 19. Movement is weighted and staggered. Faces vary and fit the moment. Creatures are shot from grass height and the kid at eye level. The first 45 s stay bright (no night or cellar before line 23). The throne room must read as a throne room: warm gold and cream stone, red-and-gold banners, a golden throne on a dais, sunlight through tall windows. **Scale is true** (ladder below). People come only from Cast v2 plus the app's King.

**Kid-safety picture rules (parent review):** the fist is only ever over the egg, never over the boy, and it never comes down. Chains land on the bunny like lassos, and it never flinches or looks hurt. The big bunny is scary but not horror: no blood, drool, red eyes or snarling at the camera. Only chains ever go in its mouth.

## Scale ladder (keep it true)
- **Bunny:** teacup (line 8), then horse (line 12), then bigger than the throne room with its ears through the roof (line 15), then bigger than the castle, towers at its shoulder (line 19).
- **Iron knight:** a head taller than the castle gate and the throne-room doors, and it fits under the throne-room roof.
- **Crown Stag:** as tall as the courtyard wall.
- **Fog Giant:** taller than the castle wall, but smaller than the bunny by line 17.
- **Oldest egg:** the size of a hill, inside a cavern under the castle.

## Reused monster models
- **Iron knight = the Black Knight model** (from "The Black Knight Knelt to Me"). Matte black iron, with an open shackle and about 3 m of chain on each wrist, one chain running to the guard captain's hands. Don't add anything that makes it read as the Black Knight character, and don't name it on screen.
- **Black stag = the Crown Stag / "Kingtaker"** (from "The Enemy's Beast Knelt to Me", Model Builder chat). Add a heavy iron collar plus chains, held by four knights. Feet lift and plant; nothing slides (Bible).
- **Giant made of fog = the Fog Giant** (from the Fog Giant film; ASSET-PATHS lists the Fog King as `town-monsters-v002\models\wellguest.glb` scaled 8-13x with gold pinprick eyes; the builder confirms which file that film used). Add an iron collar with a chain.
- All three already have approved looks, so no new still is needed for them. Only the chains are added.
