## Why it looks the way it does (from the 11-21 analysis of what went viral)
Our biggest hit (My Reflection Knelt to Me, 840K) opened on ONE huge character with a face, made of water and wearing a crown, bowing to a tiny kid under a blue sky in front of a crowd. The recent flops were dark and foggy, used small captions, and held one still shot for a long time. So:
- Bright noon daylight with a blue sky. No fog, no dusk, no dark grade.
- A new shot about every 1.5 s. No shot held longer than 3.5 s.
- Huge captions: white capitals with a thick black outline, 2-3 words at a time, centered low, matching the voice word for word.

## Script (final, word for word)
In this town, the river won't let anyone cross.
So the king built a bridge. Everyone pays him to cross it.
Even the ducks.
I'm Finn. I didn't pay.
I walked across, and the river bowed to me.
This morning, the king's soldiers took my mom.
She couldn't pay the toll. So they locked her in the castle.
I walked straight into the river.
It split in half.
And inside the water, a face opened its eyes.
A giant made of water rose up, taller than the castle.
And he bowed to me.
The whole town froze on the riverbank.
The king ran onto his bridge and screamed, "Nobody crosses without paying!"
So the giant picked up the bridge.
The king was still on it.
I said, "Relax, Your Majesty. It's all water under the bridge."
The giant dropped him in the river.
The river spun him around like a duck.
Then it spat him out in the mud.
His soldiers dropped their spears and ran.
The giant reached over the castle wall.
He lifted Mom out, like a bird from a nest.
He set her down right next to me.
Then the whole town walked across the riverbed behind us.
Nobody paid. Not even the ducks.
Mom found me in a basket on that river.
I was one day old.
She raised me all by herself.
The giant looked at her and said, "Thank you."
"For twelve years, I let no one cross."
"I was waiting for the sea's son to come home."
"You kept him safe for us."
Mom cried.
I didn't know rivers could cry. This one did.
Then the giant looked down the river, toward the sea.
"The sea is coming for his son," he said.
"And the sea is a lot bigger than me."
Do I go to the sea? A, go. B, stay with Mom.
Follow if you'd cross a river for your mom.

- **The king:** greedy and smug, with a crown and a red and gold robe. He's always on his toll bridge with a money box.
- **The River Giant:** a colossal giant made of clear, moving river water, taller than the castle, with a crown of white foam and glowing pale-blue eyes. He has a calm, ancient, kind face. Simple, blocky, readable shapes: the face must read at phone size.
