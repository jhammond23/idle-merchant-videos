## The vibe (read this first)
This one makes no sense, and that is the point. Play it completely straight.
- **One continuous moving camera, no cuts** (Jake asked for this by name). The whole video is one trip around a school yard: the camera whips from kid to kid as each one is named. The whip-pan is the punchline.
- **Over-exaggerated faces** (Jake asked for this too). Jaws on the floor, eyes popping out, hair blown back. Every reaction is too big.
- **Dave never reacts.** Half-closed eyes, bored, even as a dragon the size of the school.
- **Every animal keeps its kid.** Greg the goose still has Greg's glasses and backpack; Tina the T-rex has Tina's bow. The viewer must know who is who in a split second.
- **Letter tags on screen** every time: G = GOOSE, L = LLAMA, D = DRAGON. The rule has to read with the sound off.
- **Fast and loud:** a new animal every two to three seconds, with a pop and a goofy sound on each (honk, splash, roar that is too small for the animal). Silly, bouncy music. Drop the music out for "He was hoping for more" and "That's not an animal."
- **Bright, saturated, sunny.** A toy-box school yard. Nothing dark.
- **The voice** is a pitched-up, nasal narrator on purpose (Jake picked it). Do not correct it.
- **Loop:** the last frame matches the first.
- Kid-safe: nobody is hurt; the animals are funny, never scary.

## Script (final, word for word, approved by Jake)
In this town, there's one rule. If you don't do your homework, you turn into an animal that starts with the first letter of your name.
My friend Greg skipped spelling. Greg is a goose now.
Linda skipped math. Llama. She still can't do fractions.
My name is Dave. I checked the list. Dog. Donkey. Duck. I can live with donkey.
So I skipped my homework. On purpose.
Everyone thought I'd turn into a dog. I didn't. I turned into a dragon.
Nobody read the whole list. Dragon starts with D.
The teacher said, "Dave, sit down." I sat on the school.
Then the whole class figured it out. Nobody did their homework.
Tina is a T-rex. Karl is a kraken. We keep him in the pool. Gary is a giraffe. He was hoping for more.
The principal called our parents. Our parents are also animals. They went to this school.
My dad is a duck. He is very proud of me.
The only kid who still does his homework is Kevin. Every night. For six years.
Yesterday, Kevin forgot. Just once.
Kevin's full name is Mega Kevin. That's not an animal.
So he turned into the homework.
It's due Friday. And it's coming for all of us.
