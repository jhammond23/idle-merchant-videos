The moon is falling. It hits in four days. Everyone's packing to run. I hatched an army to protect our planet instead.
In this town, every kid gets an egg on their tenth birthday. Most hatch a spark that floats around your head. The mayor's son got a lightning bolt, and he hasn't shut up about it since.
My egg sat in the window for a year. Cold. Kids called it a rock. The mayor's son borrowed it once, as a doorstop.
This morning, it cracked down the middle. Out stepped a knight made of blue fire, taller than the castle gate. Then a giant made of storm clouds, with lightning for eyes.
The whole square went silent.
The king's plan was to hide in his basement. Mine was bigger.
First, the giant tried to blow the moon away. It didn't even wobble. He blew the roof off the bakery instead.
Then the knight threw his sword at it. It flew so high, it scratched the moon. Just a scratch. But for one second, the moon stopped.
Then the sword came back down, right through the mayor's roof.
So it can be cut. We just have to reach it.
