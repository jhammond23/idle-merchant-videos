## The series
The moon is falling, and it hits in four days. Each episode is one day of the kid's plan, and the comments vote on the next move. Day 1 ends on a discovery: the moon can be cut. The vote is A (every kid's spark makes the sword longer than the river) or B (the giant builds a staircase of storm clouds to the moon). Day 4 is the finale.

## Creatures: the "Model Builder" chat is building them
Jake asked for the creature models to come from the **Model Builder** chat. Do not build your own versions. Use theirs from `In This Town Studio\library\creatures\egg-army\` when they land, once Jake approves their stills. Until then, block your shots out with simple stand-ins. The models are:
- **The Blue Fire Knight:** a knight made of blue fire, taller than the castle gate. Armour shapes made of flame, a blue-white core, and a sword of blue flame.
- **The Storm Giant:** a giant made of dark storm clouds, with lightning crackling inside and two lightning-white eyes.
- **The burning moon:** a cratered moon with fire streaming off its leading edge. It must also work without the fire, as the plain huge moon hanging over the town.
- **Sparks:** small glowing orbs that float around kids' heads. One is a crackling **lightning bolt** for the mayor's son.
