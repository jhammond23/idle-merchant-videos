## Cast
- **The kid (lead):** about 10, one bright outfit colour nobody else wears (a burnt-orange tunic and scarf), calm and unbothered the whole film. He never fights; things come to him.
- **The mayor's son:** smug, fancy clothes, a hawk on his arm. Loses it at 36.8 s.
- **Town kids:** each with a small lizard (shoe-sized) in a pocket or on a shoulder.
- **Forty knights on horses**, each with a war hawk on the arm; **the captain** with a lance.
- **The king**, on a high balcony over the square.
- **Creatures:** a stone bear taller than the castle gate (heavy, slow, cracks of glowing orange between stones); a fox made of grey smoke, small, eyes like two lanterns, trailing wisps. A new creature model is fine here; show both in the cast check.
- **The egg:** big, dark grey, rock-like, sitting in a cottage window; cracks down the middle with orange fire inside.

## Frame 0 (cover)
The kid in the square, calm, the cracked glowing egg beside him, the stone bear towering behind him and the smoke fox at his shoulder, knights small in the background. Title: **THE DRAGON PICKED ME. I HATCHED AN ARMY.** Small tag under it: **DAY 1 · DRAGON COMES SATURDAY**. Handle top left; Flashloop card top right (sponsor edition only).

## The vibe
Power fantasy, not fighting. Nobody throws a punch. The wins come from everything in town switching to the kid's side while the people who doubted him lose what they had. Every few seconds there is a new big picture. The kid stays calm; everyone else reacts. Deep colour, strong sun, then gold evening light for the ending.
