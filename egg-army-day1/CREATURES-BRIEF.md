# Egg Army creatures: brief for the Model Builder chat (Jake, Wed 11-25, 10:21 AM)

Jake: "have the model builders build the models for the creatures." These models are for the 4-day "Egg Army" series ("I HATCHED AN ARMY TO STOP THE MOON."). **Video builder (2)** films it at **Crown Stag quality**, in Blender, in the In This Town Studio pipeline. Build them so that builder can drop them straight into the Stag town. Animals are hard to build well, so every creature here is a glowing humanoid or object spirit.

## The models
1. **The Blue Fire Knight.** A knight made of blue fire, taller than the castle gate (about 4 to 5 times a Roblox kid's height). Armour shapes in blue flame with a blue-white hot core. Glowing eye slits in the helm. A cape of flame. A long **sword of blue flame** that can be held, thrown and lengthened (Day 2 might make it "longer than the river"). Needs a rig: stand, step out of an egg, draw the sword, throw it, and stand heroically.
2. **The Storm Giant.** A giant made of dark storm clouds, huge (about twice the knight's height), with lightning crackling inside its body and **two lightning-white eyes**. Its edges are soft and billowing, but the silhouette stays a readable head, shoulders and arms. Needs a rig: rise out of the sky, inhale, blow a hurricane, lift, throw, and stand.
3. **The burning moon.** A cratered moon that can fill most of a 9:16 frame, with fire streaming off its leading edge as it falls. It also needs a calm version with no fire for the evening shots.
4. **Sparks.** A small glowing orb that floats around a kid's head (several colours), plus one crackling **lightning-bolt spark** for the mayor's son.

## Look rules
- Crown Stag quality: deep colour, strong light, real glow. Flames and lightning must read at phone size with no noise or flicker.
- Epic, intimidating and fast, never slow or cute.
- No people. Kids, the king and so on come only from the locked Reflection cast; that is not your job.
