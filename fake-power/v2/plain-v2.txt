In this town, every kid gets a fighting power at twelve, and the duke ranks us all.
The baker's kid got hot hands. The duke's captain got thunder fists, top rank.
My fists just glow like lanterns. The duke gave me a zero badge and called them fake.
He says glowing fists burn people, so zeros can't touch anyone.
Not even my little sister. She's five. She hums everywhere.
Zeros also haul the duke's boulders. We pile them under the bridge.
Every night, I punched those boulders anyway.
Today the whole town came to the bridge for the ranking.
My sister sat on the rail, humming.
Then the boulder pile under the bridge stood up. It was a stone giant.
Its first stomp knocked a beam loose, and the beam trapped my sister.
So I ran for her.
The captain stepped in front of me. "Zeros can't touch her, lantern boy."
"Watch me."
So he shoved me with his thunder fists.
His boots slid backward. I didn't move.
The baker's kid hid his hot hands.
Then the giant swung a boulder at the bridge. I caught it with one glowing fist.
One jump, and I was on the giant's arm.
I punched it the way I punch boulders every night.
The arm cracked, and the giant crashed into the river.
I ran back and lifted the beam off my sister like a twig.
Then I did the one thing zeros are never allowed to do.
I hugged her tight.
She didn't burn. She laughed.
Everyone turned to the duke.
"Arrest him!" he squeaked. Nobody moved.
Here's what the duke missed.
Every night, he made us zeros haul more boulders.
And every night, I punched them.
My glowing fists never made me strong.
His boulders did.
He laughed so hard at weak kids that he made a strong one.
Then a badge hit the ground behind me.
The captain had thrown his away. Twenty years at top rank.
He wasn't looking at the duke. He was looking at my sister.
And I was still holding her.
What would they call your power?
