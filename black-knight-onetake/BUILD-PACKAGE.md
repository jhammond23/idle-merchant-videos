## What is different from the original (only two things)
1. **The camera:** one continuous moving take, with a few hard cuts placed on purpose (listed below). Jake's words: use the continuous camera, unless there are a few specific moments where a cut or another technique helps the emotion or the excitement.
2. **The first three seconds:** the voice now opens with the brag, "In this town, the legendary Black Knight knelt to me." The picture must open on that moment too. Everything after about ten seconds is the original recording, untouched.

## The vibe (read this before anything else)
This one is **not** a comedy. It is awe, then injustice, then triumph. Play it completely straight.
- **The boy is small, quiet and stunned, then steady.** He never poses or shouts. His power is that the biggest thing in the room chose him. Keep him tiny in the frame until he lifts the sword.
- **The Black Knight is slow, heavy and silent.** About three times a man's height. Every step is an event. He never hurries and never shows his face.
- **The room does the reacting:** the laughing court, the royal kids holding their breath, the Prince's face falling, a hundred guards, then a hundred knees.
- **Light tells the story:** warm candle gold at the start; cold and blue-grey when the King speaks; washed-out for the flashback; rich gold from the moment the boy touches the sword. Deep black shadows throughout. Royal red carpet and banners.
- **Sound:** a low drone under the walk, one boom per step, a hard silence before the kneel, a bass hit on the knee, a cold string under the King, a rising gold swell on the sword, thunder on the knees. The voice track already has one soft low boom on its very first frame: leave it.
- **Pace:** uneven on purpose. Fast whips on the doors and on "Seize them both". Long slow drifts of three to four seconds on the walk, the stillness, the sword and the last picture.
- Kid-safe: swords are drawn but nobody is hurt and nobody fights.

## The camera plan
- **One moving take** from the first hard cut to the kneel, and again from "My king" to the end. The camera glides, tracks, whips and cranes; it does not cut.
- **Hard cuts are allowed only where the beats say HARD CUT: seven at most**, plus the cut into the sponsor ending. They are: the jump from the cold open to the start of the day; the three impact cuts on the kneel; in and out of the flashback; and, only if needed, the cut to the overhead view on "thunder".
- **Other techniques, each used once or twice:** slow motion (the dust at the doors, the sword lifting); a dead-still camera with silence ("he stopped"); a colour change for the flashback; a full fast circle ("Seize them both").
- At least 85% of the running time must be continuous camera.

## The look

## Script (final, word for word)
In this town, the legendary Black Knight knelt to me.
Every royal kid gets a knight on Knighting Day.
I'm the stable boy who sweeps up after their horses.
Every Knighting Day, I stand at the back of the throne room with my broom.
This year, the Prince got the Golden Knight, and nobody had seen him in over ten years.
Then he pointed at me and said, "Keep sweeping, stable boy. Maybe one day a horse will pick you."
The whole room laughed, even the King.
Then the two great doors at the end of the hall burst open!
Dust poured in, and behind it stood a shadow taller than the doorway.
It was the Black Knight.
Nobody had seen him in a thousand years.
The old story says he only comes back when it's time for the next true king to rule.
His armor was as black as a burnt pot, and every step sounded like a church bell falling down the stairs.
It was forty steps from the doors to the throne, and every step made the candles shake.
He walked past the royal kids, one by one, and none of them breathed.
The Prince stepped out and held out his hand, like the knight was coming for him.
The Black Knight walked right past him.
He kept walking, all the way to the back of the room, all the way to me.
One step away, he stopped.
Then he knelt, and the floor cracked under his knee.
He said, "My king."
The whole room gasped, but the King didn't.
He wasn't surprised at all.
He stood up and said, "I knew you would come back for him."
Ten years ago, the King took the throne from my real father.
Then he hid the baby prince where the Black Knight would never look.
The knight would look for a prince, not a boy with a broom.
The King knew who I was the whole time.
Then he shouted, "Seize them both!"
A hundred guards drew their swords, and the doors slammed shut behind us.
The Black Knight didn't fight.
He held his giant sword out to me, handle first.
It was taller than me, but when I touched it, it felt as light as my broom.
I lifted it over my head, and every knight in the room turned around.
The guards, the brand new knights, and even the Golden Knight knelt to me at once!
A hundred knees hit the floor like thunder.
The Prince was the only one left standing, next to his father.
The King spent ten years hiding me.
The Black Knight found me in forty steps.
What would your first order be as the new king?

## The planted clues and their payoffs
- The broom: he is holding it in every shot until the sword. "It felt as light as my broom." Frame the first touch of the sword the same way as his grip on the broom.

- The forty steps: the footprints he leaves on the walk are what the camera looks back at on the second-to-last line.
