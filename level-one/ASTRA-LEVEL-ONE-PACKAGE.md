## Why this video looks the way it does (from the 11-21 analysis of what went viral)
The hits were bright, fast and short. The recent flops were dark and foggy, used small captions, and held one still shot for a long time. So:
- Bright noon daylight in every outdoor shot. No fog, no dusk, no dark blue color grade. The main colors are warm gold and orange: banners, stone and sun.
- A new shot about every 1.5 s. No shot held longer than 3.5 s.
- Huge captions: white capitals with a thick black outline, 2-3 words at a time, centered low, matching the voice word for word.
- The level numbers are always big and readable on screen.

## Script (final, word for word)
In this town, the number over your head is how strong you are.
Most people are level ten.
The king's dragon is ninety-nine, the highest ever.
I'm level one.
Every year, the king puts the weakest kid in front of his dragon.
He calls it a tradition. The dragon calls it lunch.
This year, that kid was me.
Then my little sister jumped in front of me.
The dragon swung its claw at her, and I caught it with one hand.
The whole square went silent.
The dragon pushed down hard, but my feet didn't even slide.
So I grabbed its claw, and I threw it over the castle.
We heard it land behind the mountain, like a barn falling off a cliff.
I looked up at the king and said, "Sorry, Your Majesty. I guess we're not on the same level."
Then my sister held up my hand, the one with a hundred written on it in charcoal.
She writes it there every morning, because she says the stone got me wrong.
Every day, the whole town laughed at her for it.
Nobody was laughing now.
The king was so angry that he sent fifty knights to grab her.
I stamped my foot once.
The ground jumped like a dropped plate.
All fifty knights fell over like toy soldiers.
The king screamed, "He's level ONE!"
Then the old man who reads everyone's number on his stone started to laugh.
He said, "Your Majesty, my stone only counts to ninety-nine. After that, it starts over at one."
The whole town turned and looked at my hand.
My sister had been right the whole time.
I was never level one. I was level one hundred.
She just counted better than the stone.
She squeezed my hand and whispered, "I told them."
Then, deep under the castle, something opened its eyes.
A number lit up over its head.
It said one.
So who's stronger? A, me. Or B, the thing under the castle?
Follow if you'd bet on the level one.
