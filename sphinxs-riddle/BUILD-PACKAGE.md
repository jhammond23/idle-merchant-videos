## The creature: THE SPHINX (built by the Model Builder chat)
A colossal sphinx carved from ancient sandstone, taller than the castle, lying in front of a huge vault door in the mountain under the castle. It has a lion body, a calm, ageless face with a crown-like headdress, and **eyes that glow like two suns**. Under the stone are **golden wings**. When it laughs, the stone cracks off its back in slabs and two enormous golden wings burst open, wider than the square.
