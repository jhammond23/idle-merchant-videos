# The Sphinx: brief for the Model Builder chat (Wed 11-25)

For "THE SPHINX'S RIDDLE." (Video builder (2) films it at Crown Stag quality, in Blender, in the Stag town). Build it as a Lunchbox pack, the same way as the Egg Army packs.

- **A colossal sphinx** carved from ancient weathered sandstone: a lion body lying down, paws forward, taller than the castle when it sits up. Cracks, moss and erosion.
- **The face:** calm, ageless and wise (a Roblox-friendly stylised face, NOT a real person), with a pharaoh-style headdress or a stone crown.
- **Eyes that glow like two suns:** warm gold-white, with a bloom that lights the ground. They must be able to open from shut (one eye first).
- **The reveal:** a second state where the stone shell CRACKS OFF its back in slabs and two enormous **golden feathered wings** burst open, wider than the square, catching the light. Rig the wings to open.
- **Rig:** head turn, jaw and mouth for speaking, one eye opening, a laugh (head back, body shaking), wings opening.
- **Statue material:** a reusable "turned to stone" material (grey, cracked, mossy) that Video builder (2) can put on any cast rig, for the statues of people.

Deliver: push ONE still to `proofs/sphinxs-riddle/sphinx.png` (the stone sphinx with sun eyes over the square, the winged version, and a kid for scale), then keep going. Claude reviews it. Tell Video builder (2) where the pack lives.
