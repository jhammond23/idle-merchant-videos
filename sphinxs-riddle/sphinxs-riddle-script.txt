In this town, a sphinx guards the vault under the castle.
Answer its question, and everything inside is yours. Get it wrong, and you turn to stone.
The square is full of statues.
This year, the King's gold ran out. So he sent his best.
First, the Prince.
"What gets bigger, the more you take away from it?"
"My father's taxes."
Stone.
Then the General. "An army."
Stone.
The King pointed at me. "The stable boy. Nobody will miss him."
"What gets bigger, the more you take away from it?"
I shovel stables every day.
"A hole."
The vault opened.
The King came running down the steps. "Those are mine!"
The sphinx turned to him. "Then answer this."
"What can you keep, even after you give it away?"
