## THE STYLE (same as Moth Wings)
- **A new shot every 3 to 4 words:** about 85 to 95 shots, average 1.0 to 1.2 s, no shot over 1.8 s. Suggested cut times per line are below.
- **Literal pictures:** every shot shows exactly what is being said at that moment.
- **Framing:** mostly eye-level medium shots (waist up, facing camera), close-up reactions, low angles on the powerful things (the dragon, the lit dungeon), wide shots only when arriving somewhere new.
- **Camera still inside a shot** (or a very slow push). All energy from the cuts. **No shake, zooms or whips: Claude adds the shake-in, crash zooms and impact shakes in the finishing pass.**
- **Bright and saturated.** Average brightness 130 or more across the film. The dungeon is **never murky**: before the torches light it is warm orange torchlight with strong rim light on faces; the only dark shot is "Then my torch went out" (one short beat). After 33 s the whole dungeon blazes gold, so it is brighter than the town.
- **Captions** like Moth Wings: small white words lower middle, 2 to 4 at a time, one key word in red. The last line big and bold.
- **Frame 0 is already moving.** No title card in the film (the title goes on the cover and thumbnail).
- **Sound, no music bed:** torch whooshes, stone grinding, a huge "FWOOM" as nine floors of torches ignite, the deep "Master." with a rumble under it, dragon footsteps that shake dust, goblin chatter, crowd gasp then silence, a door knock.

## NEW: big cartoon faces (Jake, 11-24: "should we reverse engineer the faces from that video as well?" Yes)
The reference reads instantly on a phone because every shot has a **huge, exaggerated face**. Ours have been small and calm. For this film, make a set of **our own** expression faces in that bold cartoon style (thick eyebrows, big mouths, simple shapes), on our **rounded heads** (Jake's rule: rounded heads stay, face art is flexible). Do not copy the reference's faces; draw ours in that spirit. At least these 10:
1. smug half-smile with one raised eyebrow (the torch boy's signature look after 33 s)
2. calm, confident, eyes half closed (the stick-it line)
3. deadpan, flat mouth ("I told him I'd think about it")
4. wide-eyed wonder, open mouth (when the torches light)
5. shocked "O" mouth, eyebrows up (the crowd, the knights)
6. terrified, teeth showing, sweat drop (the prince in the dungeon)
7. sneering bully grin (the prince, "Keep the monsters busy")
8. sheepish, embarrassed (the prince at the door next morning)
9. goblin mischief grin
10. kind, soft smile (the torch boy leaving the torch at the gate)
Use one per shot, and change the face every time the feeling changes.

## Cast, creatures and places
- **The torch boy (me):** the locked-cast `kid` (burnt-orange shirt, `hero_crop` hair, scale 0.8), plus a small leather torch satchel. Personality: quiet, kind, then **cool and cocky** once the dungeon obeys him. He never fights. He stands still and things obey.
- **The prince:** the locked-cast Prince from Reflection, in shining armour with a gold cape. Arrogant at first, terrified in the dungeon, legs like jelly in the square, sheepish at the door.
- **Fifty knights:** locked-cast guards in silver armour with banners.
- **Goblins:** small, green, big ears, mischievous and comic, not scary. Rounded heads.
- **The dragon:** **the hero creature of this film. Make it amazing.** As big as the church. Obsidian-black scales with glowing ember-orange cracks, long horns, bright gold eyes, a spiked tail. When it obeys, it lies down at his feet and rests its chin on the floor **like a big dog waiting for a walk** (tail thumping, dust puffing). It must be a real model with real animation (walk, lie down, climb stairs, rise out of the gate), never a pop-in.
- **The dungeon:** nine floors in a huge round shaft under the castle, each floor a ring of arches and torches, monsters in every arch (slimes, skeletons in silly hats, bats, a cyclops, mimics), stairs spiralling down. Before 33 s: warm torchlight, deep shadows. After 33 s: **every torch on every floor ablaze**, gold light filling the shaft top to bottom.
- **The castle and town square:** our bright medieval town at midday, the castle on top, the dungeon gate in the square (a big iron-and-stone arch with a single torch bracket beside it).
