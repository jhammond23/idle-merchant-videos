## Script (final, word for word)
In this town, everyone is born with a string on their wrist.
The other end is tied to the monster you're meant to fight.
I'm Fold. Mine goes straight up, into the clouds.
Most strings go to a rat in the cellar. The king's went to a dragon. That's why he's king.
For ten years, mine has been pulling tighter.
On your tenth birthday, you pull your monster in and fight it. Win, and it's yours.
That's today.
The whole town came to watch. From behind the wall.
The king kept his dragon close. Just in case.
I pulled.
It pulled back. My feet left the ground.
Dad grabbed my ankles. The captain grabbed his. Then the butcher.
Forty people were holding on, and we were all still going up.
We passed the church roof. The butcher said a word I'm not allowed to say.
Then the clouds opened, and we saw it.
It was bigger than the town.
And it wasn't diving at us. It was climbing, as hard as it could.
The captain yelled, "That thing isn't attacking. It's running!"
From what? There was nothing up there but us.
Forty people were hanging off my legs. If that string snapped, we all fell.
I had never pulled as hard as I could. I never had a reason.
So I pulled.
It came down out of the clouds like a kite.
It landed across the whole valley, and it would not look at me.
The king's dragon tried to hide behind the king.
Then every string in town went tight. All at once.
The rats. The wolves. The bear. The dragon.
Every monster in the kingdom was pulling away from me.
Ten years, I thought mine was dragging me to a fight.
It was trying to get away.
To the biggest thing in the sky, I'm the monster.
Even Biscuit pulled away. Dad's rat. He's slept on my pillow since I was born.
He had never seen me pull.
What do you think I am?
