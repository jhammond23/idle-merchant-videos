Jake approved this cast "for now"; it will be replaced later. **Use it for every film started from now on.** It replaces the 16-character Model Builder brief (`CAST-LIBRARY-V2-BRIEF.md`), which is cancelled: there's no Model Builder job.

## Rules
- **Mix them.** No two people in a shot should share a face style, skin and body. Use the yellow characters in crowds too.
- Use every face's expression set (Pokemon-style: 13 expressions plus talk and blink files in the town-anime-faces pack; classic faces: smile, grin, smirk, shocked, scared, angry), so faces match the moment.
- The Studio Bible's evening notes still apply: steady cameras, eased movement, staggered timing, believable faces.
