# Cast Library v2: a reusable, diverse cast (Jake, Wed 11-25, 10:37 PM)

**Builder: "Model Builder".** Build it as Lunchbox packs, one per character, in `Lunchbox\material\story-video\cast-v2\`, plus a `README.md` that lists every character with its file names and the roles it suits.

## Why
Jake: our cast "is not diverse enough." In the big reels in our niche, the cast looks very different from one person to the next: some share a style, others are a completely different style, the body shapes differ, and the Roblox avatar styles differ. **Some are the classic yellow avatars, others are regular skin tones.** Viewers should tell every character apart at a glance on a phone, even in a crowd.

## Rules
- **Every character must be different from the others in at least three of these:** body type, skin, face style, hair, outfit colour, and size.
- **Bodies (mix them):** classic blocky R6; slim, tall R15; wide and chunky; small kid; very tall adult; muscular.
- **Skin (mix them):** classic bright-yellow Roblox skin, and a full range of real skin tones (light to dark).
- **Faces (mix them):** classic Roblox face decals (the simple smile, the smirk, the chill face, the big grin), anime-style faces, and the Moth Wings line-art faces. Every character gets an **expression set**: neutral, smug, happy/laughing, shocked, scared, angry, crying and determined. The expressions must read as believable on a phone.
- **Heads stay rounded** (Roblox-style rounded heads, no sharp cubes), per Jake's 11-24 rule. Head size and shape can vary with the avatar style.
- **Medieval fantasy wardrobe**, because our stories are medieval: tunics, armour, robes, crowns, aprons, cloaks. Add one or two everyday-modern variants per character only if they're cheap to make.
- Textured clothing (Studio Bible, 11-24). Every character is rigged and has idle life (breathing, blinking) so it never looks frozen, plus offset idle timing so a crowd never moves in sync.
- **Keep our current lead:** the burnt-orange hero kid (`kid`, from the locked cast) stays as character 1, so the old films still match.
