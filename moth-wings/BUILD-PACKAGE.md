## THE STYLE (measured on the reference, frame by frame)

| | Reference | What you build |
|---|---|---|
| Shots | 57 in 47 s | **about 55 in 50 s** (the shot list below) |
| Average shot | 0.82 s | **0.8 to 1.0 s**. No shot over 1.6 s except the last one |
| Brightness | 154 of 255 | **140 to 155** average. Bright midday sun, blue sky, white clouds |
| Camera inside a shot | almost still | **Still, or a very slow push-in.** No pans, swings, whips or shake. All the energy comes from the cuts |
| Framing | eye-level medium shots | **60% eye-level medium** (waist up, facing camera, a pose or a big face), **25% close-up reactions**, **10% low angle** on the powerful thing, **5% wide**, only when arriving somewhere new |
| What a shot shows | exactly the word being said | **Literal.** "Sparrow wings are weak" is a kid with little sparrow wings looking weak. Every noun gets its own picture |
| Captions | small white text, lower middle | **Small white captions** with a thin dark outline, centred at about 62% of the height, 2 to 4 words at a time, timed to the words. **One key word per caption in red** (listed in the shot list). The **final line is big and bold**, the size of a title |
| Frame 0 | already moving, no title card | **No title card.** Frame 0 is shot 1, already moving. The title goes on the cover and thumbnail only |
| Sound | music bed | **No music bed** (our rule). Wing flaps, whooshes on the big cuts, crowd laughter, a gasp, wind on the cliff, a deep boom on the reveal |

**Our own style on top:** the locked Reflection cast (`C:\Users\jake2\Downloads\roblox\story-videos\shared-assets\locked-cast\README.md`) with **rounded heads**, textured clothes, our medieval town and cliffs, saturated colour. Handle `@idlemerchantrng` top left. Make two editions as usual (sponsor and clean), but only for the full film, not the test.

## Cast and props
- **Me (the hero):** the locked-cast `kid` (burnt-orange shirt, `hero_crop` hair, scale 0.8). **Moth wings:** small, grey, soft and fuzzy, the size of his hands, with two faint eye spots. At the reveal they open to many times his height, dark grey with glowing pale eye spots, big enough to cast the whole cliff into shadow.
- **The duke's son:** a locked-cast body, a rich purple and gold tunic, `hero_slick_back` hair, a smug face. **Griffin wings:** huge, golden, feathered, wider than a wagon.
- **The first-year:** a small kid (scale 0.6), `hero_pixie` hair, scared face, tiny sparrow wings.
- **Crowd kids:** locked-cast crowd members with sparrow wings (small, brown), eagle wings (big, brown and white) and a few griffin wings.
- **Faces:** any face drawing from our earlier reels is allowed (laughing, shocked, scared, smug), on rounded heads. Never invent a new person.
- **Places:** the town square on Wing Day (banners, a stage), and the Sky Festival on a high grassy cliff (bunting, kites, the castle far behind, open sky, a long drop).

## Order of work (Jake's rule: small steps, checked each time)
1. **Cast still:** push `proofs/moth-wings/cast-check.png`: me with moth wings (closed, then fully open), the duke's son with griffin wings, the first-year, three crowd kids (sparrow, eagle, griffin), side by side with a frame of the Reflection hero. Rounded heads.
2. **Test clip:** push `proofs/moth-wings/test-clip.mp4`: **shots 1 to 14 only (0 to 15.11 s)** with the real voice, captions and sound. Run `glitchscan` on it.
3. **Stop and wait for Claude's review**, in `moth-wings/REVIEW-NOTES.md` on this branch. Claude measures your shot lengths, brightness and captions against the reference and accepts, or sends exact notes. Iterate until accepted.
4. Only after the test clip is accepted **and** "CAST OK moth-wings" is in the README: build the full film, both editions, cover and posting thumbnail, to `ready/moth-wings/` on main.

## Check before every push
- Average shot 0.8 to 1.0 s; count them (`ffmpeg ... select='gt(scene,0.25)'`).
- Average brightness 140 or higher.
- Every shot shows what the words say at that moment.
- No pans, swings, whips or shake. No glitches, flashes or strobing. 1080x1920, 30 fps.
