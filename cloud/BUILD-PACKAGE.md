## Script (final, word for word)
In this town, every kid gets a cloud. Tomorrow at noon, the king shoots mine down.
When your cloud opens, your creature climbs down. See the numbers? That's how long each one took.
The baker's kid got a wolf. One day.
My little sister, Nell, got a hawk. One day.
The mayor's son got a griffin. One month.
And the king's dragon took one whole year. The longest cloud in history.
And yeah. You spotted me already. The kid getting rained on.
That's my cloud. Ten years. Still counting.
You're thinking: it's empty.
So does the whole town. They call me Rain Boy.
Everyone except Nell. Every morning, she throws three pebbles up at it. "Anybody home?"
Nobody ever is.
And here's the law. A cloud still shut on your tenth birthday gets shot down. In the square. At noon.
I turn ten tomorrow.
But hold on. You're doing the math right now.
One day for a wolf. One month for a griffin. One year for a dragon.
So what takes ten?
In this town, you're not allowed to ask that.
That night, my cloud stopped raining. For the first time in ten years. Something was awake up there.
So at sunrise, I ran. Rooftops. The bridge. Twenty guards behind me.
It's hard to hide when you bring your own weather.
They caught me at the river.
Noon. The whole town in the square. My cloud right above it.
And under it, the king's crossbow. The bolt was taller than a house.
Nell broke through the guards. She threw three pebbles. "Anybody home?"
Nothing.
The crossbow fired.
The crack broke every window on the square.
And then... the bolt came back down. In pieces.
My cloud didn't have a hole in it.
Nobody moved.
Then, from inside the cloud...
Boom... boom... boom.
Up on the castle wall, the king's dragon dropped flat on its belly.
And the king whispered: "Not another one."
What do you think is up there?
