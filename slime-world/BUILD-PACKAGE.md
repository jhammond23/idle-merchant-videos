## The one idea
**Every time the slime is hit, it doubles in size.** His name is Tiny. He is a baby and he is scared. He gets so big that the town ends up inside him, and then the people punching to get out make him grow by themselves. One more hit and he is bigger than the world. The only thing that shrinks him is a real apology, so the whole kingdom, king last, has to say sorry to a small boy's pet.

## The vibe (read this before anything else)
**A deadpan comedy that turns warm.** The narrator never raises his voice. The pictures are what is funny.
- **Tiny is adorable the whole time.** Two big round eyes, a small smile, a wobble. He never looks angry and never attacks anyone. Being huge is something that is happening TO him. When he is scared, his eyes get bigger and he shakes.
- **Everyone else is slow to learn.** Tobin, the guards and the king all do the one thing that makes it worse. Play them straight-faced.
- **Inside the slime is calm, pretty and silly.** Green lake light, slow floating, muffled sound. A horse that does not care. A king pretending he meant to do this.
- **Nobody is hurt, ever.** Kicks are cartoon boots that bounce off. Spears bend like rubber. Everyone can breathe inside. Everything he swallowed comes back fine, just damp.
- **The heart of it** is 81 to 90 s: "He is a baby. And he is scared of you," then the hug. Play that completely straight and slow. No joke on screen there.
- **Colour:** bright and saturated. Deep blue sky with white clouds, green hills, warm stone, red and yellow bunting. Tiny is a clear lime green that glows a little. **I am in a bright red tunic in every shot** so I never get lost against green or stone. Do not use haze or a tan, washed-out grade.
- **A real, lived-in world:** a full town square with stalls, flags, a stage and a crowd; streets with people in windows; a castle on a hill; mountains behind it. The size jokes only work if there are plenty of ordinary things around to measure him against.

## Making the size a fun watch
This is the point of the video. Please put real care here.
1. **Always give him something to stand next to.** Every new size gets a familiar object in the same shot: my hand, the wolf, a cow, Tobin, a house door, rooftops, the castle, a mountain, another kingdom, the world.
2. **The camera keeps losing the fight.** For hits 1 to 8 it backs away a little more each time to hold him in frame. At hit 9 it runs out of room and has to tilt up. At hit 12 he rolls over it. After that only huge, steady, far-away shots can hold him.
3. **A sound that grows with him.** Every doubling is a *BWOOP* with a jelly wobble, and each one is lower and bigger than the last, until at hit 23 it is a slow, deep whale note you feel more than hear. Shrinking is the same idea backwards: a soft *bloop* that climbs in pitch.
4. **A hit counter on screen.** Bottom left, like a punch counter in an arcade game: **HIT 1**, **HIT 2**, and so on, stamped in on each doubling. From 74 s it reads **26 / 27** in red. In the apology scene it runs back down. It is the clock people will watch.
5. **The LVL 1 tag never changes size.** It is big over an apple and a speck on top of a mountain. Twice, let the camera hunt for it and find it.
6. **Things carry on inside him.** A clock tower still ticking. Washing still on a line, floating. A market stall with a customer still paying. Chickens.
7. **Scale ladder to follow** (storybook sizes, do not do real maths on screen):

| Hit | Tiny is the size of | Stand him next to |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | an apple | my hands, the eggshell |
| 1 | a dog | Tobin's wolf |
| 2 | a cow | a cow |
| 3 | a house door, looking down | Tobin |
| 4 to 8 | a cottage, then a church | stalls, the stage, the guards |
| 9 | taller than the houses | rooftops, chimneys, pigeons |
| 12 | the whole street, dome-shaped | the square, seen from the far end |
| 15 | bigger than the castle | the castle on its hill |
| 19 | bigger than the mountains | a snowy range, an eagle |
| 23 | covering other kingdoms | a desert town far away |
| 26 | a quarter of the world | the planet, from space |

## Jake's camera rule (please follow it exactly)
Jake said fast camera swings at the start of a video "keep jerking around" and make people leave.
- **0 to about 35 s: steady shots joined by hard cuts.** No whips, no fast swings, no snap zooms anywhere in the film.
- **After that: smooth, slow, gliding camera**, especially inside the slime, where everything floats.
- The far-away shots at hits 15, 19, 23 and from space are steady shots joined by hard cuts, like a nature film. That is on purpose.
- If a move would have to be fast to work, make it a cut instead.

## Cast and set
Use the Lunchbox faces, hair and outfits.
- **Me:** about ten, small, messy hair, a **bright red tunic**, bare arms. Quiet. He is the only person who never hits anything.
- **Tiny:** a see-through lime-green slime with two big glossy eyes and a small smile. Soft highlights, a gentle inner glow, a constant slow wobble. He must still read as cute and as the same character at every size, so keep the face the same friendly shape and let it get bigger with him. If there is no good slime in the Lunchbox catalog, ask the Model Builder for one before you start: a rigged slime with an idle wobble, a squash-and-pop doubling, scared eyes, happy eyes, and a see-through body other models can sit inside.
- **Tobin:** about twelve, big, smug, expensive clothes. **His wolf:** grey, friendly, more curious than fierce.
- **The King:** tall, crown, heavy robe, very straight back. Proud, never frightened, always pretending things are under control. **His dragon:** large, red or gold, level 99, and it ends up floating inside Tiny looking embarrassed.
- **The town:** a baker in an apron, guards with spears, a horse, a cow, chickens, a crowd of townspeople of all ages.
- **Egg Day:** eggs of every size and colour on a long table and in kids' arms. If the earlier egg videos' eggs are on the PC, reuse them.

## Script (final, word for word)
In this town, every kid gets an egg, and whatever hatches is yours for life.
Its level floats over its head the second the shell cracks.
Tobin's egg was the size of a barrel. A wolf. Level forty.
The king's egg took four men to carry. A dragon. Level ninety-nine.
My egg fit in one hand.
It cracked. A slime. The size of an apple. Level one.
The whole square laughed.
I named him Tiny.
Tobin kicked him.
He doubled.
Now he was the size of a dog. Still level one.
Tobin kicked him again. A cow.
Tobin is not a fast learner.
He stopped laughing on the third kick, because Tiny was looking down at him.
The guards came with spears. Every spear that touched him made him twice as big.
The guards are not fast learners either.
Hit nine. Taller than the houses.
Hit twelve. He rolled over the whole street, and we were all inside him.
You can breathe in there. Everything is green and slow, like the bottom of a lake.
A horse floated past me. It seemed fine with it.
Then the king, still on his throne, acting like this was the plan.
Everyone inside started punching to get out.
And every punch was a hit.
Hit fifteen. Tiny swallowed the castle.
Hit nineteen. The mountains.
Hit twenty-three. His shadow fell on kingdoms that had never heard of us.
I did the math. At hit twenty-seven, Tiny is bigger than the world.
We were at twenty-six.
The king drew his sword to cut his way out.
I swam in front of him.
"Stop hitting him."
"It's a monster, boy."
"He's level one. He's a baby. And he's scared of you."
Way down in the middle, I could still see him. The real one. Apple-sized. Shaking.
I swam down and held him.
He only gets smaller one way. Somebody has to say sorry, and mean it.
So the whole kingdom lined up, inside a slime, to apologize to my pet.
The baker. The guards. Tobin, twice. The first one didn't count.
Every sorry cut him in half.
The king went last. He said, "Mistakes were made."
Nothing happened.
He tried again. On his knees.
The mountains came back. Then the castle. Then our street.
Now Tiny is the size of an apple again. Almost.
Tonight he tried to climb back into his shell.
He doesn't fit.
Somebody didn't mean it.
Who do you think it was?
