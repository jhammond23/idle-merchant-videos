In this town, every kid gets an egg, and whatever hatches is yours for life.
Its level floats over its head the second the shell cracks.
Tobin's egg was the size of a barrel. A wolf. Level forty.
The king's egg took four men to carry. A dragon. Level ninety-nine.
My egg fit in one hand.
It cracked. A slime. The size of an apple. Level one.
The whole square laughed.
I named him Tiny.
Tobin kicked him.
He doubled.
Now he was the size of a dog. Still level one.
Tobin kicked him again. A cow.
Tobin is not a fast learner.
He stopped laughing on the third kick, because Tiny was looking down at him.
The guards came with spears. Every spear that touched him made him twice as big.
The guards are not fast learners either.
Hit nine. Taller than the houses.
Hit twelve. He rolled over the whole street, and we were all inside him.
You can breathe in there. Everything is green and slow, like the bottom of a lake.
A horse floated past me. It seemed fine with it.
Then the king, still on his throne, acting like this was the plan.
Everyone inside started punching to get out.
And every punch was a hit.
Hit fifteen. Tiny swallowed the castle.
Hit nineteen. The mountains.
Hit twenty-three. His shadow fell on kingdoms that had never heard of us.
I did the math. At hit twenty-seven, Tiny is bigger than the world.
We were at twenty-six.
The king drew his sword to cut his way out.
I swam in front of him.
"Stop hitting him."
"It's a monster, boy."
"He's level one. He's a baby. And he's scared of you."
Way down in the middle, I could still see him. The real one. Apple-sized. Shaking.
I swam down and held him.
He only gets smaller one way. Somebody has to say sorry, and mean it.
So the whole kingdom lined up, inside a slime, to apologize to my pet.
The baker. The guards. Tobin, twice. The first one didn't count.
Every sorry cut him in half.
The king went last. He said, "Mistakes were made."
Nothing happened.
He tried again. On his knees.
The mountains came back. Then the castle. Then our street.
Now Tiny is the size of an apple again. Almost.
Tonight he tried to climb back into his shell.
He doesn't fit.
Somebody didn't mean it.
Who do you think it was?
