## Script (final, word for word)
In this town, when the king's bell rings, everybody has to kneel.
Your knees bend by themselves.
Even the king's horse has to kneel. The horse hates it.
I'm Fold, and my knees have never bent.
I just pretend, so the king won't find out.
Today, the king put in his gold earplugs and rang the bell once.
He said, "Stay down until you pay me double."
My grandma's knees are bad. She started to shake.
So I stood up.
A thousand people were on their knees, and one kid was standing.
The king screamed, "Guards! Grab him!"
But the guards were kneeling too.
One said, "We'd love to, Your Majesty."
So I walked to the bell tower. Nobody could stop me.
The king ran up the stairs behind me.
At the top, I had a choice.
Two rings lets everyone stand up, so I could ring it twice.
But tomorrow, he'd just ring it again.
So I pulled the plugs out of his ears.
Then I rang the bell.
The king's knees bent.
He knelt on his own tower, in front of his whole town.
I said, "Stay down until you say sorry."
He said it. He said it loud.
Then I rang it twice, and a thousand people stood up.
I helped Grandma up first.
She hugged me and said, "Nobody decides when you bend but you."
I cut the bell rope and took it home.
Grandma hangs her laundry on it now.
But that night, another bell rang, far past the mountains.
It was bigger than ours.
And for the first time in my life, my knees bent.
Do I go find that bell, or stay away? A, find it. B, stay away.
Follow if you'd have stood up too.
