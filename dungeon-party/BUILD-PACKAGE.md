## Script (final, word for word)
In this town, nobody enters the dungeon without a party. And every party in town told me no.
The knights said I was too small. The mages said I was too slow. The thieves just took my lunch.
So for three years, I carried their bags to the dungeon door, and waited outside.
Then my little sister got sick. The only cure grows on the bottom floor, and no party has ever made it that far.
So I went in alone, with a lantern and a bread knife.
Floor one. Forty goblins looked at me, and stepped aside.
Floor five. A troll held the door.
I didn't know why. I just kept walking.
On the bottom floor sat the Dungeon King. He was as big as a church, and he was smiling.
He said, "A thousand years, they've come in fours and fives to kill me. You're the first one brave enough to come alone."
He handed me the cure. Then he said, "Is your party full?"
We walked out together. He carried my bag.
Every party in town was standing at the door.
The knight captain said, "Kid. Got room for one more?"
I looked him up and down. "Sorry. You're too small."
Then the Dungeon King leaned down and whispered, "There are six more dungeons. And their kings are lonely too."
