## Script (final, word for word)
In this town, the statues blink when somebody lies.
The king hates them. He lies a lot.
I'm Fold, and I got the king to lie to a hundred statues at once.
It started when the prince pushed me in the fountain.
He said, "I never touched him."
The stone knight behind him blinked.
Everybody saw it, and everybody laughed.
The next day, the king said, "My grain house is empty, so I'm taking your bread."
He said it indoors, where there are no statues.
Then he sent men with hammers to smash every statue in the square.
The first one in line was my mom's.
She pulled six kids out of a fire, so the town carved her in stone.
I stood in front of the hammer.
The man said, "Move, kid."
I said, "I'm not scared of you."
Behind me, my mom's statue blinked.
I was lying. I was very scared. I stayed anyway.
Then people started standing next to me.
One, then ten, then the whole square.
The king came out to see what was wrong.
Now he was standing in front of a hundred statues.
So I asked him, loud, "Is the grain house empty?"
He looked at the statues. He looked at the crowd.
He said, "Yes."
A hundred statues blinked at once.
It sounded like a hundred doors closing.
The baker yelled, "Open the grain house!"
It was full to the roof.
The king shouted, "I am still your king!"
Every statue blinked again. Even his own.
So I asked, "Then who is?"
A hundred stone heads turned, and looked at the same person.
It was Mara, the old woman who sells bread.
She shook her head at me and whispered, "Don't."
Do I tell the town, or keep her secret? A, tell them. B, keep it.
